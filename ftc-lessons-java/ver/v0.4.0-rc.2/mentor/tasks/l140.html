140 lesson: make the sticks mean the field¶

Push the stick away from you and the robot goes away from you. It does that whichever way the robot happens to be facing, so a driver never has to think about which end of the robot is the front.

Before you start.

  • L135 is done.

  • AdvantageScope is installed on the same laptop, and a gamepad is plugged into the laptop.

  • The robot, the Driver Station, and about 2 m by 2 m of clear floor for the last step.

Where the driver stands. “Away from the driver” is the field’s +x from the Red Alliance Area, and -x from the Blue. From the Red Alliance Area, the robot starts at heading 90°, facing +y, so at the start a push away from the driver slides it to its right.

the steps¶

L140S010: copy the two starters into mytry¶

This lesson has two files. L140FieldRelativeOpMode is the teleop. L140AutoOpMode is L135’s auto with a choice to do nothing, and it hands the teleop the alliance. Both use L135’s menu.

Copy. In lessons, copy L140FieldRelativeOpMode and L140AutoOpMode into mytry, one at a time.

Check.

  • mytry holds L140FieldRelativeOpMode and L140AutoOpMode.

L140S020: switch your copies on¶

Change. In each copy, delete the line @Disabled, under the comment that names this step.

Check.

  • In each, under the step’s comment, the next line reads // 'public' so the robot can run this OpMode. 'public' allows access from any code.

L140S030: drive and turn at once, the L110 way¶

The starter drives the way L110 did. Forward on the stick is forward for the robot.

Simulator. In the Terminal, run:

./gradlew :TeamCode:simRun --args="mytry.L140FieldRelativeOpMode --pad"

AdvantageScope. Connect to the simulator as in L020, click 2D Field, and open the Localizer folder, then pinPoint.

Check.

  • Push the left stick a little forward and the right stick left, and hold both.

  • The robot drives in a small circle, and x_in and y_in stay close to where they started.

  • It goes where its nose points, and its nose keeps turning.

L140S040: turn the stick backwards through the heading¶

You drive from the Red Alliance Area, as in the picture. From now on, fieldXSpeed means a speed along the field’s +x. It could be away from you or toward you, depending on which Alliance Area you are in. fieldYSpeed means to your left, along the field’s +y, when you are in the Red Alliance Area. fieldYSpeed means to your right, along the field’s +y, when you are in the Blue Alliance Area. On AdvantageScope’s 2D Field, +x runs to the right and +y runs up.

The wheels only know the robot’s own forward and sideways. L120 turned a step on the robot into a step on the field, through the heading. This turns the other way, from the field to the robot, so the signs on sin swap.

Change. Press Ctrl+C in the Terminal to stop the simulator. In driveFromTheSticks(), find the comment that names this step. Delete the line drivetrain.sticks(fieldXSpeed, fieldYSpeed, turnCcwSpeed); under it, and type:

        // When on L140S040, see
        // https://coding.stitt.cc/ftc-lessons-java/guide/tasks/l140.html#l140s040
        // for what to do here.
        double heading_rad = follower.pose().heading();
        double sinHeading = Math.sin(heading_rad);
        double cosHeading = Math.cos(heading_rad);
        double robotForwardXSpeed = fieldXSpeed * cosHeading + fieldYSpeed * sinHeading;
        double robotStrafeYSpeed = -fieldXSpeed * sinHeading + fieldYSpeed * cosHeading;
        Tracker.publish("drivetrain/robotForwardXSpeed", robotForwardXSpeed);
        Tracker.publish("drivetrain/robotStrafeYSpeed", robotStrafeYSpeed);
        drivetrain.sticks(robotForwardXSpeed, robotStrafeYSpeed, turnCcwSpeed);

The follower knows which way the robot faces. The drivetrain does not. So the OpMode asks the follower, and does the turn itself.

Simulator. Run the same command again, and connect AdvantageScope.

Check.

  • Hold the same two sticks. The robot spins, and at the same time it travels to the right on the 2D Field, along +x.

  • x_in climbs while heading_deg goes around and around.

L140S050: give the teleop a menu¶

The teleop gets L135’s menu, with two rows. Alliance says which end of the field you drive from. Start says where the robot starts: where the auto ended, or against the wall, for a run with no auto first. OpModeStorage.alliance is the alliance the auto chose. L140S090 makes the auto set it.

Change. Stop the simulator. Near the top of the class, under the comment that names this step, type:

    // When on L140S050, see
    // https://coding.stitt.cc/ftc-lessons-java/guide/tasks/l140.html#l140s050
    // for what to do here.
    L135AutoMenu menu = new L135AutoMenu(
            new L135Row("Alliance", "Red", "Blue"),
            new L135Row("Start", "Auto End", "Wall"));

At the end of init(), under the comment, type:

        // When on L140S050, see
        // https://coding.stitt.cc/ftc-lessons-java/guide/tasks/l140.html#l140s050
        // for what to do here.
        menu.startAt("Alliance", OpModeStorage.alliance);

In init_loop(), under the comment, type:

        // When on L140S050, see
        // https://coding.stitt.cc/ftc-lessons-java/guide/tasks/l140.html#l140s050
        // for what to do here.
        menu.loop(gamepad1);

In loop(), under the comment, type:

        // When on L140S050, see
        // https://coding.stitt.cc/ftc-lessons-java/guide/tasks/l140.html#l140s050
        // for what to do here.
        Tracker.printToDs(menu.summary());

Simulator. Run it with --init, as in L135:

./gradlew :TeamCode:simRun --args="mytry.L140FieldRelativeOpMode --init --pad"

Check.

  • The Terminal shows > Alliance: < Red >, then Start: < Auto End >, then R-A.

  • Press the D-pad’s right, then A. The last line reads B-A.

L140S060: turn the sticks round for the Blue Alliance Area¶

fieldXSpeed = -leftStickY; and fieldYSpeed = -leftStickX;, under the comment that names this step, are right for the Red Alliance Area. From the Blue Alliance Area, away from you is the field’s -x. Your left is -y. A push away makes leftStickY negative. So for blue, fieldXSpeed is leftStickY with no minus. The same goes for fieldYSpeed.

Change. Stop the simulator. In driveFromTheSticks(), find the comment that names this step. Delete the lines under it, down to the comment that names L140S040, and type:

        // When on L140S060, see
        // https://coding.stitt.cc/ftc-lessons-java/guide/tasks/l140.html#l140s060
        // for what to do here.
        double fieldXSpeed;
        double fieldYSpeed;
        String alliance = menu.chosen("Alliance");
        if (alliance.equals("Red")) {
            fieldXSpeed = -leftStickY;
            fieldYSpeed = -leftStickX;
        } else {
            fieldXSpeed = leftStickY;
            fieldYSpeed = leftStickX;
        }

        fieldXSpeed = speedShaped(fieldXSpeed, bandToSetToZero);
        Tracker.publish("drivetrain/fieldXSpeed", fieldXSpeed);
        fieldYSpeed = speedShaped(fieldYSpeed, bandToSetToZero);
        Tracker.publish("drivetrain/fieldYSpeed", fieldYSpeed);

        double turnCcwSpeed = -rightStickX;
        turnCcwSpeed = speedShaped(turnCcwSpeed, bandToSetToZero);
        Tracker.publish("drivetrain/turnCcwSpeed", turnCcwSpeed);

        // When on L140S040, see

Each speed is made first with no value, then given one in whichever half of the if runs. Then each speed is shaped as in L080 and published, and the turn is read from the right stick.

Simulator. Run the same command again, and connect AdvantageScope.

Check.

  • Press Enter with R-A last, and push the left stick away from you. The robot drives to the right on the 2D Field, along +x.

  • Stop it and run it again. Press the D-pad’s right, then A, for B-A, then Enter.

  • Push the left stick away. The robot drives to the left, along -x, and x_in falls.

L140S070: start against the wall¶

poses() already makes the red alliance’s poses. Like L135’s, it turns them for blue.

Change. Stop the simulator. In start(), under the comment that names this step, type:

        // When on L140S070, see
        // https://coding.stitt.cc/ftc-lessons-java/guide/tasks/l140.html#l140s070
        // for what to do here.
        String startPlace = menu.chosen("Start");
        if (startPlace.equals("Wall")) {
            PoseFactory poses = poses();
            Pose wall = poses.of(72.0, botStartY_in, 90.0);
            follower.setPose(wall);
        }

In poses(), under the comment, type:

        // When on L140S070, see
        // https://coding.stitt.cc/ftc-lessons-java/guide/tasks/l140.html#l140s070
        // for what to do here.
        String alliance = menu.chosen("Alliance");
        if (alliance.equals("Blue")) {
            chosenPoses = redPoses.rotateAround(72.0, 72.0, 180.0);
        }

Simulator. Run the same command again, and connect AdvantageScope.

Check.

  • Press the D-pad’s down, then right, then A. The last line reads R-W.

  • Press up, then right, then A. The last line reads B-W.

  • Press Enter. The robot is at the middle of the blue wall: x_in 72, y_in 133.5, and heading_deg 270.

L140S080: drive it facing you¶

Robot. Stop the simulator. Send the code, and run L140 Field Relative from the Driver Station. Press PLAY with R-A last. Stand off the robot’s left side, so that away from you is the field’s +x.

Check.

  • Push the right stick until the robot’s nose points at you.

  • Then push the left stick away from you. The robot drives away from you while it faces you.

  • Turn it a quarter turn and try again, and the stick still means the same way on the field.

L140S090: hand the alliance from the auto to the teleop¶

At a match, the drive team chooses the alliance once, in the auto. OpModeStorage holds values that last from one OpMode to the next, such as where the auto ended. At the top of L140AutoOpMode, preselectTeleOp = "L140 Field Relative" has the Driver Station choose the teleop when the auto ends.

Change. In L140AutoOpMode’s start(), under the comment that names this step, type:

        // When on L140S090, see
        // https://coding.stitt.cc/ftc-lessons-java/guide/tasks/l140.html#l140s090
        // for what to do here.
        String alliance = menu.chosen("Alliance");
        OpModeStorage.alliance = alliance;

OpModeStorage turns red. Click it and press Option+Enter.

Robot. Send the code. Run L140 Auto from the autonomous list. During INIT, choose Blue and leave the auto at N-Nothing, so the last line reads B-N. Press PLAY, and let the auto run until it stops on its own, after 30 seconds.

Check.

  • The Driver Station now has L140 Field Relative chosen.

  • Press INIT. Its menu’s last line reads B-A.

  • Stand off the robot’s left side and push the stick away from you. The robot drives away from you.

What’s happening. E141 and E142 show why your two lines work. E143 shows the field from each Alliance Area.

No gamepad. --args="mytry.L140FieldRelativeOpMode --left_stick_y=-0.4 --right_stick_x=-0.7" holds both sticks, for the circle in L140S030 and the picture at the top. Before L140S040, the run was watched at x_in 70.812 and y_in 7.230 after about 8 s.

From L140S050 on, --init --dpad_right=true --a=true --left_stick_y=-0.4 chooses B-A and holds the stick away; after Enter, x_in went from 72 to -3.89 in one run.

After L140S040, the robot drifts toward +y as it goes: one run went from x 72 in and y 10.5 in to x 179 in and y 43.5 in, about 17 degrees off a straight line. The simulator has no walls, so a run left going drives off the field.

If it didn’t, and LessonsTest.l140_fieldRelativeIgnoresWhichWayTheRobotFaces fails with same stick, still moves away from the driver expected:<0.0> but was:<1.0>, the line still reads drivetrain.sticks(fieldXSpeed, fieldYSpeed, turnCcwSpeed);.

If it didn’t, and it fails with expected:<-1.0> but was:<1.0>, the turn runs the wrong way. The minus sign is missing from -fieldXSpeed, or both signs on sinHeading are the other way around.

If it didn’t, and it fails with same stick, still moves away from the driver expected:<0.0> but was:<-0.3338674754196965>, the heading went in as degrees. Math.cos wants radians.

If it didn’t, and LessonsTest.l140_theTeleOpMenuChoosesTheAllianceAndTheWall fails with starts at the alliance the auto handed over: Live NetworkTables on. AdvantageScope: connect to 192.168.43.1, the menu’s lines from L140S050 are missing.

If it didn’t, and LessonsTest.l140_fromTheBlueAllianceAreaTheStickMeansTheOtherWay fails with away from the blue drivers is -x, behind a robot facing +x expected:<-1.0> but was:<1.0>, blue’s fieldXSpeed still has its minus.

If it didn’t, and it fails with their left is -y, to the right of a robot facing +x expected:<-1.0> but was:<1.0>, blue’s fieldYSpeed still has its minus.

If it didn’t, and LessonsTest.l140_theAutoHandsItsAllianceToTheTeleOp fails with the alliance handed to the teleop expected:<[Blue]> but was:<[Red]>, the lines from L140S090 are missing from the auto’s start().

The Driver Station chooses the teleop only when the auto ends on its own or its 30 seconds run out; pressing STOP leaves it unchosen. The drive team still presses INIT and PLAY for it, and its menu works during INIT, as the auto’s does.

What you just did. Field relative is one turn of two numbers and nothing else. The same sticks, the same mix, the same scaling. All that changed is which pair of numbers goes in.

It also depends on the follower knowing which way the robot faces. If the heading drifts 20 degrees, the sticks are 20 degrees crooked. This is the first lesson where a mistake somewhere else in the robot shows up in the driver’s hands.

Where next. L150 puts the robot’s own directions back on a bumper, so a driver can have both.
