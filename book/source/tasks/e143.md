# 143 what's happening: the field from each alliance area

Here you learn where Pedro's x and y lie on this season's field. You also see how they look from
each Alliance Area. In L140, they are why pushing the stick away from you drives the robot away
from you. That holds when you drive from the Red Alliance Area.

In [L140](l140.md), the follower keeps track of where the robot is. It uses Pedro's x and y. This
page shows them on this season's field.

```java
double heading = follower.pose().heading();
```

From above, with the audience at the bottom, (0, 0) is the corner where the red wall meets the
audience's wall. +x runs from the red wall toward the blue wall, and +y runs away from the
audience. The far corner is (144, 144), in inches. A heading of 0 faces +x.
